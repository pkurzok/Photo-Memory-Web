# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 13 October 2026*

Photo Memo+ is a free app for iPhone and iPad, made by Peter Kurzok, with an optional one-off Pro purchase. This page explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

There is no account and no login. We run no server of our own. The single exception is a picture set you deliberately choose to share, which is encrypted on your device before it goes anywhere — that is described in full under *Sharing picture sets* below.

## Your photos

The pictures you choose are copied into the app’s own storage on your device, so that a board still works after the original has been moved or deleted. **That copy stays on your device.** It is not uploaded, not shared, and not visible to us — unless you yourself share a picture set, which is the one case described below.

The app asks iOS for access to your photo library only at the moment you pick pictures, and iOS decides what it hands over. Removing the app removes its copies with it.

The player names you type, your high scores and your settings are stored on the device in the same way, and are never uploaded.

## Sharing picture sets

When you open the share sheet for a picture set, the photos in that set are packed up and **encrypted on your device**. Only the encrypted copy is uploaded, to our iCloud (CloudKit) container. The key that opens it travels only inside the link you send, in the part of the address that is never sent to any web server — so we cannot read what you share, and neither can anyone we host with.

The encrypted copy is kept for up to **7 days** and then deleted. Anyone you give the link to can open it during those 7 days; after that the link stops working and the copy is gone.

This happens when the share sheet **opens**, so a set is uploaded even if you close the sheet without sending it. That copy is deleted on the same 7-day schedule as any other.

Sending needs an iCloud account on your device. **Receiving needs none** — no account, no sign-in. No analytics event ever carries the link, the key, or the photos.

## Advertising — Kickstart Exchange

The free version can show a single small banner below the board, supplied by **Kickstart Exchange**. It promotes other independent apps. Buying Pro removes it.

The banner reports which advert was shown and whether it was tapped. That is **not tied to your name, your Apple Account or any identifier we could use to recognise you**, and it is not used to track you across other companies’ apps or websites. It never includes your photos, your picture sets or the player names you type.

Kickstart Exchange’s own privacy notice: [https://exchange.kickstart.tools/privacy](https://exchange.kickstart.tools/privacy)

## Crash reporting — Sentry

If the app crashes, a crash report is sent to **Sentry** so the fault can be found and fixed. A report describes the state of the program — which code failed, on which iOS version and which device model. It contains none of your photos and none of your game data.

Crash reporting is controlled by **Crash Reports** in the app’s Settings. It is **on by default**. Switching it off shuts the Sentry SDK down immediately, without a restart, and nothing further is sent.

Sentry’s own privacy policy: [https://sentry.io/privacy/](https://sentry.io/privacy/)

## Usage statistics — Mixpanel

Anonymous usage statistics are sent to **Mixpanel** to show which parts of the app are actually used. The events are things like *which screen was opened*, *which board size was chosen*, *which table background was picked*, *how many players were playing*, and whether sound or haptics are switched on. They are not tied to a name or an account.

These events **never include your photos and never include the player names you type.**

One exception is worth stating plainly: if you deliberately send feedback from Settings, the message you write is transmitted along with it. That happens only when you choose to send it.

Usage statistics are controlled by **Data Analytics** in the app’s Settings. This is likewise **on by default** and can be switched off at any time.

Mixpanel’s own privacy policy: [https://mixpanel.com/legal/privacy-policy/](https://mixpanel.com/legal/privacy-policy/)

## Purchases

Photo Memo+ is **free to download and free to play**. Photo Memo+ Pro is a single purchase that removes the banner and lifts the free tier’s limits. The app also offers optional tips, which unlock nothing — a tip is a tip.

Anyone who bought Photo Memo+ while it was a paid app keeps every Pro feature, free and forever, with nothing to buy and nothing to restore.

Every purchase is handled by Apple. Payment details are never seen by us and never reach the app; we only learn that a purchase succeeded.

Apple’s privacy policy: [https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## Children

The app plays with the pictures that are already on the device, and with any picture set someone shares with you. There is no user-generated content, no chat and no link out to the open web from inside the game. The free version shows one banner advertising other apps, described above; Pro removes it.
