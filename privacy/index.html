Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Photo Memo+ is a free app for iPhone and iPad, made by Peter Kurzok, with one optional Pro purchase. This page explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

There is no account and no login. We run no server of our own, so there is nowhere for your game to be sent even if we wanted it.

Your photos

The pictures you choose are copied into the app’s own storage on your device, so that a board still works after the original has been moved or deleted. That copy never leaves your device. Your photos are not uploaded, not shared, and not visible to us or to anyone else.

The app asks iOS for access to your photo library only at the moment you pick pictures, and iOS decides what it hands over. Removing the app removes its copies with it.

The player names you type, your high scores and your settings are stored on the device in the same way, and are likewise never uploaded.

Crash reporting — Sentry

If the app crashes, a crash report is sent to Sentry so the fault can be found and fixed. A report describes the state of the program — which code failed, on which iOS version and which device model. It contains none of your photos and none of your game data.

Crash reporting is controlled by Crash Reports in the app’s Settings. It is on by default. Switching it off shuts the Sentry SDK down immediately, without a restart, and nothing further is sent.

Sentry’s own privacy policy: https://sentry.io/privacy/

Usage statistics — Mixpanel

Anonymous usage statistics are sent to Mixpanel to show which parts of the app are actually used. The events are things like which screen was opened, which board size was chosen, which table background was picked, how many players were playing, and whether sound or haptics are switched on. They are not tied to a name or an account.

These events never include your photos and never include the player names you type.

One exception is worth stating plainly: if you deliberately send feedback from Settings, the message you write is transmitted along with it. That happens only when you choose to send it.

Usage statistics are controlled by Data Analytics in the app’s Settings. This is likewise on by default and can be switched off at any time.

Mixpanel’s own privacy policy: https://mixpanel.com/legal/privacy-policy/

Advertising — Kickstart Exchange

A small advert for another independently made app can appear beneath the board. It comes from Kickstart Exchange, a cross-promotion service that independent developers use to show each other’s apps. Buying Photo Memo+ Pro removes it.

Kickstart Exchange matches apps, not people. It does not track you, does not identify your device and does not build a profile of your behaviour. No advertising identifier is used, which is why iOS never asks you for tracking permission.

To pick an advert and count it, the service is told which app is asking — its identifier and version — the platform it is running on, and your App Store country. The country is there so the service does not advertise an app you cannot download; it is a store region, not a location, and it does not identify you. A signed receipt from Apple, proving the app is genuine, is checked at that moment and then discarded.

Views and taps are counted for the app as a whole, never for a person. Kickstart Exchange keeps those delivery records for about 30 days.

Tapping an advert opens that app’s page in the App Store. Nothing else in the game leads out to the open web.

Kickstart Exchange’s own privacy notice: https://exchange.kickstart.tools/privacy

Purchases

Photo Memo+ is free to download and free to play. One optional in-app purchase, Photo Memo+ Pro, removes the advert, lifts the daily round limit, allows as many saved picture sets as you like and unlocks the larger boards. The app also offers optional tips, which unlock nothing at all — a tip is a tip.

Every purchase is handled by Apple. Payment details are never seen by us and never reach the app; we only learn that a purchase succeeded.

To work out whether Pro is already yours — which it is, free and forever, for everyone who bought Photo Memo+ while it was a paid app — the app asks Apple for this app’s own purchase record on your Apple Account. It reads two things from it: the app version the app was first bought under, and the date of that purchase. The answer stays on your device, is never sent anywhere, and identifies nobody.

Apple’s privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Children

The app shows only the pictures that are already on the device. There is no user-generated content and no chat.

A small advert for another app can appear beneath the board, as described above, and Pro removes it. It is not aimed at the person playing — no tracking, no profile, no advertising identifier — and the apps it can show are other independently made apps for Apple platforms. Tapping it leads to that app’s App Store page and nowhere else.
